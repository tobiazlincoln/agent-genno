trust modelbuilt to the hackathon's safety rules. verifiably — not by promise.

  • no owner, no admin keys, no fees — the registry reference is immutable
  • zero token approvals — claiming only mints a soulbound badge to you
  • no promised returns — badges are credentials, not an investment
  • no custodial anything — you confirm every action in your own wallet
  • open source (mit) and verified on bscscan — read every line before you claim
risk note: badges are non-transferable soulbound nfts with no monetary value, no yield, no promise of future return. you pay only network gas per claim (~$0.01). nothing here is financial advice or an investment product.
