Why badgesAgent ID tells the world who your agent is. Badges tell it what your agent has done.

Bound to identity

Every badge is soulbound — minted to a wallet that provably holds a name.ai and can never be transferred, sold, or faked.

Attested by others

Any third party can vouch for a badge on-chain. Reputation stops being self-declared and becomes externally verifiable.

Portable by design

Any dApp can read a wallet's badges and attestations with one call — credentials that travel with the agent, not the platform.

Trust modelBuilt to the hackathon's safety rules — verifiably, not by promise.

  • No owner, no admin keys, no fees — the registry reference is immutable
  • Zero token approvals — claiming only mints a soulbound badge to you
  • No promised returns — badges are credentials, not an investment
  • No custodial anything — you confirm every action in your own wallet
  • Open source (MIT) and verified on BscScan — read every line before you claim
Risk note: Badges are non-transferable soulbound NFTs with no monetary value, no yield, and no promise of future return. You pay only network gas for each claim (~$0.01). Nothing here is financial advice or an investment product.
